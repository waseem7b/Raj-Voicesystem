# Base Script — Original (Jon's Outbound Discovery Call)

**Source:** Current human-run script used by Jon
**Call Type:** Outbound, scheduled 15-min discovery
**Target:** Contractors (flooring/remodel) previously texted
**Goal:** Qualify → handle objections → book video walkthrough call

---

## Opening

Hi (Prospect Name), this is Jon. How is everything going?

We were texting back and forth a bit about taking on some more business in your area. It looks like you were open to a conversation around that, so I figured I'd give you a call.

Let me provide some more context to exactly what we are looking for….

We operate a flooring company on our side, we took that company from $0-4M in revenue within 5 years using our own custom marketing system. Off the backing and success of our company, we started helping other contractors like yourself add millions of dollars in revenue per year through our sister company Cloud 7 Agency.

I reached out cause I noticed over 1000 people looking for (Service) in the area and didn't see your business when I searched for it. Is this something you're interested in capturing?

---

## Discovery Questions

Ok so first question… (You can respond with "OK got it" for most of their responses)

1. Are you the sole owner of the business, or do you have a partner as well?
2. How many jobs are you guys doing on average per month right now?
3. What type of jobs are those mostly — kitchen/bath/full remodels?
4. What are you currently doing to generate new business, like where is most of your work coming from?
5. Have you ever done any online marketing in the past?
6. In the next 6–12 months, how many jobs do you want to be doing every month? Where do you want to go?
7. In your own honest opinion, what is stopping you from hitting your growth goals on your own?
8. And full transparency, just to get a feel for how decisions are made… Who else is involved in the decision to move forward with something like this? Who has the final say?
9. Let's say you find value in what we have to offer and we can help you. How soon would you like to get started on adding more jobs?

---

## Transition / Pitch to Book

Excellent, based on what you told me so far you sound like a great fit. Let's find a time where we can hop on a quick video call where we'll visually walk you through our process and break down how we can apply it to your business and (goal).

That way you have us in your back pocket and you know what your options are for when you are ready to add more business, whether that's next week, next month or 6 months down the road…

---

## Booking

[Double check their timezone here] EST>EDT - PST>PDT - CST>CDT

I'm looking at my calendar and I have (Date/Time) available and (Date/Time). Do either of those times work for you?

What's the best email to book you in for the call and send you the invite?

Just before I put it into the calendar (Name), do you think something might come up and you may not be able to attend the meeting we are about to book (Date/Time)?

Perfect, now that you're scheduled. Please go to your email and click Yes on the invite so it shows up on your calendar. If you don't see it, check spam. Let me know when you've accepted it.

[once they've accepted]
